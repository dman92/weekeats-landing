How it works

From “what's for dinner?” to a full fridge in three steps.

1

Tell it what you cook

Just type the names of your usual dishes, or pick from a catalog of ideas. No recipes to copy, no forms to fill in.

2

Tap “Plan my week”

WeekEats spreads your dishes across lunch and dinner, so no two days look the same. Move anything with a tap.

3

Go shopping

Your shopping list is already written: every ingredient, quantities added up, grouped by aisle. Ready to share.

Your week

See your week the way you like it.

Swipe day by day on a beautiful board, or see the whole week at a glance in a simple list. Your choice, one tap away.

  • Real life, not a template. A soup and a main for lunch? Breakfast only at the weekend? Add as many dishes and meals as each day needs.
  • Colour for every meal. Breakfast, lunch, dinner and snack each have their own colour, so the day reads in a second.
  • No dates to manage. A week is just Monday to Sunday, ready to reuse whenever you want.
Shopping list

A shopping list that writes itself.

Plan the week and the list is done. Change a dish and the list follows. You never type “onions” again.

  • Quantities added up across every dish of the week, in grams, units or cloves.
  • Grouped by aisle, so you walk the supermarket once.
  • Knows why you need it: each item shows the dishes it's for. Add anything else by hand.
New · Plan together

One kitchen. One week. Everyone on the same page.

Invite your partner, your family or your flatmates. You all see the same week, the same dishes and the same shopping list, and every change shows up for everyone right away.

  • Invite with a 6-letter code. Send it by any app; they type it and they're in.
  • Up to 4 people per home, everyone can plan and tick things off the list.
  • Whoever's at the shop sees what's already been bought. No more double yoghurt.
ELS Same week, same list, in real time.
New · Saved weeks

Keep the weeks that worked.

A light week, a summer week, a training week… Save as many as you like and switch between them in one tap. The shopping list follows the week you pick.

  • Start empty or from a copy of a week you already have.
  • See each week at a glance: its dishes, and which days are planned.
Dishes

Type a name. Get the whole dish.

Write “chickpea curry” and WeekEats brings the ingredients, the quantities and a photo. Ask for the steps whenever you're ready to cook.

  • Your way. “With tofu, no aubergine”: tell it how you make it and the recipe adapts.
  • Ideas when you're stuck, from a catalog of dishes with photos, filtered for vegan or vegetarian homes.
  • Prefer your own recipe? Write it yourself; it's yours to edit any time.
